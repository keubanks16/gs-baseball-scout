How it works

  1. Add a clip and tap the batterClaude reads the GameChanger scoreboard so the at-bat is filed under the right hitter.
  2. GS Baseball Scout tracks the swingA pose model follows 17 points on the batter's body, finds leg lift, foot down, hip and shoulder turn, and looks for the ball off the bat.
  3. It makes the call before you tag itPull, middle or opposite field, from that hitter's history and from swings that looked like this one.
  4. Tap where the ball wentEvery tagged at-bat sharpens the spray chart, the defensive positioning and the next prediction.
