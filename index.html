How it works

  1. Add a clip and tap the batterClaude reads the GameChanger scoreboard so the at-bat is filed under the right hitter.
  2. GS Baseball Scout tracks the swingA pose model follows 17 points on the batter's body, finds leg lift, foot down, hip and shoulder turn, and looks for the ball off the bat.
  3. It makes the call before you tag itPull, middle or opposite field, from that hitter's history and from swings that looked like this one.
  4. Tap where the ball wentEvery tagged at-bat sharpens the spray chart, the defensive positioning and the next prediction.

Scout a whole game

  1. Get the game on your phoneIn GameChanger, play the game full screen in landscape and record it with iPhone Screen Recording (turn on Do Not Disturb first). Only the innings the other team bats are needed. For your own games, Live Stream and Record to Device saves the whole game.
  2. Tap the four bases onceThat tells GS Baseball Scout where the field is, so it can turn the ball's flight into a spot on the spray chart.
  3. Let it runIt watches the runner's lane to find balls in play, then checks each one: the batter, the swing and the ball off the bat. Keep the app open and the phone plugged in.
  4. Check and saveNames come from the GameChanger scoreboard. Fix anything that looks off, then save them all to the spray charts.
